# Pi Tasks Terms of Use

Pi Tasks is an independent prototype for lawful micro-tasks and testing. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pi Network.

- Post only tasks and work you are authorized to share. Do not ask for credentials, private information, unlawful work, or payments outside the app.
- Posting a reward does not escrow or guarantee any funds. Demo payments and claimer payouts are simulations. Optional Testnet funding goes to the app, not the claimer, and has no Mainnet value. Do not use this prototype to arrange work that depends on a real payment.
- Task history is stored in the current browser and may be lost when site data is cleared. The service is provided as-is, without assurance of availability or durable records.

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for information about local storage and the optional Pi integration.
